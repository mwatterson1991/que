Frequently Asked Questions

How does Que work?

Que replaces your standard alarm with a guided audio session. When your alarm fires, instead of a jarring sound, you hear a calm, purposeful hypnotherapy-style session designed to meet you in the theta state — that half-asleep, half-awake window where your brain is most receptive. The result: you wake up primed, not panicked.

Why didn't my alarm go off?

  • Make sure notifications are enabled for Que in your device settings.
  • On iOS, check that Que is allowed to send Time Sensitive notifications (Settings > Que > Notifications).
  • On Android, confirm that Que has the "Alarms & reminders" permission (Settings > Apps > Que > Permissions).
  • If your phone is in Do Not Disturb or Focus mode, make sure Que is listed as an allowed app.

Can I use Que without the audio session?

Que is built around the audio experience — that's where the value is. You can choose "Silence" as your background sound if you prefer voice only, but we recommend giving the full session a chance for at least a week.

How do I change my background sound?

Open the app, go to Settings, and select your preferred ambient sound. Options include gentle rain, ocean waves, morning birds, singing bowl, and theta binaural tone. Your selection applies to every session.

What is Que Premium?

Premium unlocks the full program library and lets you create custom sessions tailored to your specific goals. Free users get three high-quality programs that rotate daily — enough to build the habit. Premium goes deeper.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iOS: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Que > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Que > Cancel.

You'll keep access through the end of your billing period.

Does Que work with Apple Watch or Wear OS?

Not yet. Que is designed for iPhone and Android phones. We may explore wearable support in future versions.

Is my data private?

Yes. Que stores your preferences (name, wake-up time, selected program) to personalize your experience. We do not sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
