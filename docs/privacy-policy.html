Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 20, 2026

Que ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address, first name, and a password. Your password is cryptographically hashed — we never store or see it in plain text.

Profile data. You may optionally add a username, bio, or avatar image. These are visible only to you unless you choose otherwise.

Alarms and preferences. We store the alarms you create (labels, schedule, selected audio program) and your app preferences (notification toggle, haptic feedback, sound selection, dark mode setting) so they sync across sessions.

Chat messages. When you interact with the in-app chat to set alarms or ask questions, those messages are stored in your account. Message processing happens locally on your device — no conversation text is sent to third-party AI services.

Progress data. We track category progress, session streaks, and activity history so you can see your growth over time.

Voice input. If you use the voice input feature, speech recognition is handled by your device's native speech engine (Apple Speech or Android Speech Services). We do not record, store, or transmit raw audio. Only the transcribed text is used, and only to parse your alarm request.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or calendar.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver the service. Your account, alarms, and preferences power the core app experience.
  • Personalize audio sessions. Your name and selected programs let us tailor your morning sessions.
  • Send notifications. If you grant permission, we send alarm and reminder notifications through Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). We do not use notifications for marketing.
  • Improve the app. We may review aggregated, anonymized usage patterns (never individual data) to improve features.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Que:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) — hosted in the United States. Supabase encrypts data at rest and in transit. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — used solely to deliver alarm notifications you have scheduled.

If we introduce additional third-party integrations (such as AI-generated audio via ElevenLabs for premium features), this policy will be updated before those features go live. Premium audio generation would only process the specific goal text you provide — never your full account data.

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). Our database enforces row-level security — each user can only access their own records. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We follow the principle of least privilege: we only collect what we need to make the app work.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you maintain an active account. You can delete your account at any time from within the app. When you delete your account, all associated data — profile, alarms, messages, preferences, activity history, and progress — is permanently and automatically removed from our systems. There is no recovery period; deletion is immediate and irreversible.

You can also clear your chat history independently without deleting your account.

7. Children's Privacy

Que is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (see Section 6).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or via email before the changes take effect. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
