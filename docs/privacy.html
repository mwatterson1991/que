Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 19, 2026

Que ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What We Collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, used to create your account and personalize your morning sessions (e.g., "Good morning, Michael").
  • Preferences: Your selected program, preferred wake-up time, and ambient sound choice. These are stored so your alarm works correctly.
  • Subscription status: Whether you are on the free tier or Que Premium, managed through Apple App Store or Google Play billing.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version. Used solely for troubleshooting support requests.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect health data, location data, or contacts.
  • We do not track your browsing activity outside the app.
  • We do not record or store audio from your microphone beyond the moment of voice-command processing, which happens on-device.

How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver your personalized morning audio sessions.
  • To manage your alarm schedule and notification delivery.
  • To process your subscription through Apple or Google's payment systems.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Que uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Data is stored securely on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech audio generation for premium custom sessions only. When you create a custom session, your session prompt is sent to ElevenLabs to generate audio. No personal information beyond the session text is transmitted. ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription billing is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We do not see or store your payment details.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We use row-level security policies to ensure users can only access their own data.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated usage data may be retained for product improvement.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Update your personal information within the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

Que is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@queapp.co.
