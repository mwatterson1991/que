Good morning. A word of grace for the day ahead. This is the day that has been made for you; rejoice and be glad in it. Weeping may endure for a night, but joy comes with the morning. The sun rises new every day and so do His mercies. You were not given a spirit of fear, but of power, of love, and of a sound mind. Be still, and know. Walk today without anxiety, for tomorrow will worry about itself. You are held, you are known, and you are deeply loved. Go gently into this morning.
