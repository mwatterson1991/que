Good morning. Wisdom from the Stoics to start your day. At dawn, when you have trouble getting out of bed, remember: I rise to do the work of a human being. Why was I made, if not for this? You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength. Waste no more time arguing about what a good person should be. Be one. It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste much of it. Begin at once. The obstacle in your path becomes the path. Today, what stands in the way becomes the way.
