# App Store Description — Morning Que

> Category: Health & Fitness (primary) / Productivity (secondary)
> Character limit: 4,000

---

Your morning sets everything. Morning Que makes sure it counts.

Most alarm clocks attack you awake. You flinch, you snooze, you start the day already behind. Morning Que does the opposite — it meets you in the moment right after waking, when your mind is still open and unguarded, and fills that window with something worth hearing.

That half-asleep, half-awake state isn't wasted time. Neuroscience calls it the theta state — a brief window where the brain is measurably more receptive to new ideas, suggestions, and reframing. Hypnotherapists have used this window for decades. Morning Que puts it in your pocket.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Set your alarm like any other clock app
2. When the alarm fires, Morning Que plays a short guided audio session — calm, focused, and built around what you're personally working on
3. You wake up with direction instead of dread

Each session layers a professional voice guide over ambient sound — gentle rain, ocean waves, morning birds, binaural theta tones, or silence if you prefer. You choose the background that feels right, and it stays consistent across every session.
