# App Store Subtitle — Que

> Limit: 30 characters (including spaces)
> Appears directly below the app name in search results and on the product page.
> Apple guidelines: no generic descriptions, no competitor names, no pricing.

---

Wake Up With Purpose

---

**Character count:** 20

**Rationale:**

- Captures the core promise — Que replaces chaotic mornings with intentional ones.
- "Wake Up" grounds it in the alarm category so users know what the app does.
- "With Purpose" elevates it above standard alarm apps without drifting into soft wellness language.
- Short enough to display fully on all device widths, leaving headroom under the 30-char limit.

**Alternates considered (documented for Michael's review):**

1. "Own Your First Five Minutes" — 27 chars. Specific and punchy, but less immediately clear to someone browsing.
2. "Morning Audio for Your Mind" — 27 chars. Descriptive, but leans generic.
3. "Alarm. Guided. Personal." — 25 chars. Rhythmic, but fragmented style may not land in search.
