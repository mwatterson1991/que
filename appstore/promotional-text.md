# App Store Promotional Text — Que

> Limit: 170 characters. Shown above the description on the App Store listing.
> Can be updated at any time without submitting a new build — use it for seasonal hooks, feature launches, or A/B testing messaging.
> Apple guideline: no pricing, no ranking claims ("#1 app"), no calls to download.

---

Your alarm fights you awake. Que does the opposite — it meets you in that half-asleep window and primes your mindset before the day starts.

---

**Character count:** 141

**Why this works:**

- **Opens with contrast.** "Your alarm fights you awake" names the problem every user already feels. It earns the next sentence.
- **Introduces the product without jargon.** "Half-asleep window" is plain language for the theta state — accessible to someone who has never heard of hypnotherapy.
- **Ends on a benefit, not a feature.** "Primes your mindset before the day starts" tells you what you get, not how the tech works.
- **Stays in Que's voice.** Direct, confident, no filler words. No "transform your mornings" or "wellness journey."

**Alternatives considered:**

1. "Most alarms shock you awake and you hit snooze. Que meets you in the moment between sleep and awake — and uses it to set your mind right." (139 chars) — good but "set your mind right" felt slightly preachy.
2. "Stop dreading your alarm. Que turns that groggy half-asleep moment into the most productive minutes of your day." (113 chars) — shorter, punchier, but "most productive minutes" oversells without explaining how.
3. "Que replaces the jarring alarm with guided audio that reaches you while your mind is still open — right at the edge of sleep." (124 chars) — explains the mechanism but doesn't land the benefit clearly enough.

**Seasonal swap ideas (for future updates):**

- New Year: "New year, same alarm. Or: wake up to something that actually changes your day. Que is guided audio for the moment your mind is most open."
- Feature launch: Lead with the new feature, e.g. "Now with custom sessions — tell Que what you're working on and wake up to audio built for you."
