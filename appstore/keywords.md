**Character count:** 97

**Rationale:**

The keywords target three search intents — alarm users looking for a better wake-up, self-improvement seekers, and people exploring guided audio/meditation.

- **alarm** — primary product function; high-volume search term in the category.
- **morning** — reinforces the wake-up context and pairs with "routine" searches.
- **hypnotherapy** — Que's core method; lower competition, high intent from users who already understand the concept.
- **mindset** — captures the personal-development audience without resorting to generic "wellness" language.
- **wake** — natural companion to "alarm"; users search "wake up" variations frequently.
- **meditation** — high-volume adjacent term; Que's guided sessions overlap with what meditation-seekers want.
- **habit** — targets users searching for habit-building tools; maps to Que's daily-use model.
- **focus** — covers the Focus & Productivity program category; strong search volume.
- **affirmation** — targets the morning-affirmation audience directly.
- **theta** — niche but high-intent; users searching for theta-state content are Que's ideal audience.
- **sleep** — adjacent to the alarm category; users browsing sleep tools often want a better wake-up experience too.
- **routine** — captures "morning routine" searches; strong crossover with the self-improvement audience.
- **calm** — emotional benefit keyword; resonates with the anti-jarring-alarm positioning.

**Keywords deliberately excluded:**

- "wellness," "journey," "self-help" — too generic and misaligned with Que's voice.
- "productivity" — Apple category name; already applied via the secondary category.
- "health," "fitness" — Apple category name; already applied via the primary category.
- "que" — the app name is already indexed; repeating it wastes characters.
- "guided" — considered but cut for space; "meditation" and "hypnotherapy" imply guided content.
